## Post 2 — 7 Unusual LinkedIn Questions Answered
*Posted: 2 days ago*
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alicjasmin_i-get-very-unusual-questions-about-linkedin-activity-7470059481541029888-vcDo

I get very unusual questions about LinkedIn.

Here's 7 of my favorite. (with answers)

Note: I'm answering each one based on my own experience (was #1 LinkedIn creator in the world), 1,000+ people coached, and 100,000+ students overall between my Link Up community and live talks.

Save this. Repost it. Or just read it. ♻️

1. "Are hashtags in posts still the main thing?" Nope, they never were. Forget about them. It's not 2023.

2. "Will tagging big creators improve my reach?" Maybe, but only if they respond. And even then, no guarantees. Linky has a pretty good "spam-tag" filter.

3. "Should I continue posting memes? They bring me the most engagement." If that engagement results in business, then I can't tell you No. If not, please stop - half the memes here are not one bit funny.

4. "I cannot send any more than 100 connection requests per day. Why?" Dude, why would you?! Follow and Connect are 2 separate functions for a reason.

5. "Are engagements pods wo..." No. NO!

6. "How can I automa...?" You can't. LinkedIn strictly prohibits automation - quote - "of any kind". Read your User Agreement if you don't believe me.

7. "How can I sound more like you in my content?" I can't tell you how many times I've been asked this. Why do you want to sound like someone else?! Seriously. Embrace your you-niquess. ❤️

Bonus:

"Can you comment on my posts, Jasmin?"

Imagine thinking it's rational I can comment on 365,000+ peoples' posts in the feed. I've been averaging 1,000+ comments per week for years - still can't respond to everyone. Not physically possible. Thank you for asking tho! 🙏

Hope you enjoyed reading these. Cheers!

## Post 5 — Breaking Every LinkedIn "Rule"
*Posted: 3 weeks ago*
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alicjasmin_i-successfully-broke-every-rule-on-linkedin-activity-7462811687214129152-YPF6

I successfully broke every "rule" on LinkedIn.

After 360,000 followers, these are the facts:

1. "Post daily" - No. Big no. In all my years, I've never once posted every day of the week. And voilà, look. Still growing. Still running a business in 75+ countries.

2. "Post minimum 3-4 per week" - No. This is my first post in a week. Sometimes life gets busy. It's okay.

3. "DM your profile viewers daily" - Sure. But I get so much inbound, I never have to "sell". I mostly message my favorite commenters, not prospects.

4. "Leverage the current trend" - Heh. No. I was the last person to try carousels in 2023. Last to try video in 2024. Don't use AI much. I set trends, I don't follow.

5. "Share vulnerable stories" - I did. I wrote 2 "I'm a solo dad" posts. Both went disgustingly viral. And I realized I never want to use that part of me for personal branding again. So yes - be vulnerable. But know your limits.

TL;DR:

1. Never post daily.
2. Skip it entirely if busy.
3. DM favorites, not prospects.
4. Stop making every trend happen.
5. Get personal, but don't "overshare".
