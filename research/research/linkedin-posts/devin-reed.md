## Post 2 — AI and Authenticity in Content
*Posted: 1 day ago*
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/devinreed_honestlyitsnotai-activity-7470130337860190209-Zenz

Lego made a World Cup ad so good that people swore it was AI. And the internet lost it. 

They featured soccer legends Messi, Ronaldo, Mbappé, and Vinícius Jr. together. 

"No way that's real! Has to be AI!!"

Messi finally captioned it hashtag#HonestlyItsNotAI just to get people to stop.

And that says everything about where we are right now.

We don't hate AI. We hate when it gets between us and something real.

But we’re also hungry for craft. We want to know the creator put their time in — because they're asking for ours. Seems fair to me.

There’s no hiding that we use AI. I could pretend I write every word from scratch. I don't.

I use it to spot patterns in what's working, pitch ideas, and polish drafts so they sound like me. 

(I've used Stanley for months to spot ideas, track progress, and draft LinkedIn posts. It learns my voice and shows me exactly which content pillars are doing well and which aren't. Link if you're curious: https://lnkd.in/gyyd8Chw.)

If AI doesn't help me sharpen my voice, then it’s getting in the way. And I cut it. 

Turns out the highest compliment in 2026 is "that's so good it must be fake."

Do you care if someone uses AI — or just that the content is good?

## Post 3 — Website Visitor ID + Content Workflow
*Posted: 6 days ago*
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/devinreed_b2bmarketing-zoominfopartner-activity-7468333488560095232-rJXJ

I've never done website visitor ID before. And I'm low-key nervous about it.

De-anonymizing someone and then cold emailing them feels... risky. Like I could easily cross the line into creepy territory.

(Then again, I used to send ice cold emails to people who didn't know me *at all*.)

But I also don't want to waste the traffic I'm generating from content.

So here's the workflow I'm setting up with ZoomInfo:

Instead of doing sales outreach to everyone who hits my site (the obvious trap), I'm trying a "Godfather play" — make an offer they can't refuse.

The idea:
↳ Visitor hits my site
↳ ZoomInfo identifies + enriches them
↳ Based on their persona, I send a relevant content asset

Because ZoomInfo gives me the context to know 𝘸𝘩𝘰 they are, not just 𝘵𝘩𝘢𝘵 they visited.

Example: 

If a Director of Content Marketing lands on my page  → they get my content strategy deck template and guide.

I shan't pitch. 
No "let's hop on a call." 
Just good ol' fashioned value.

My goal is to start a conversation or get them into my newsletter. Def not assume they're ready to buy anything.

I'm sharing this because I genuinely don't know if it'll work. And I'd rather build this in public than pretend I have all the answers.

I'll report back in 30 days with what happens. 

Have you done something similar?

What's worked? What would you do differently?
