## Full Transcript

This is Cameron. He quit his six-figure tech job to build software while traveling 
the world making more money than any business could ever pay him. But what will 
take Cameron from where he is right now to scaling faster is content. And this is 
how his software will get the distribution he needs for free while building 
authority and getting leads.

My name is Lara Acosta. I built three, six, and seven figure businesses using 
content and LinkedIn. I built my personal brand from zero to 300,000 followers 
and I know exactly what the bottlenecks in content strategy are and how to fix 
them today.

The key elements we need to cover in order to build a profitable brand are:
1. Expertise and how we can own it
2. Relevant stories and how we can use them to drive attention
3. Traffic generation and how to manufacture it

**Positioning First**

We start with what you want to be known for, then your positioning, then the 
social proof that goes with it.

High value skills = what you can tell stories about AND educate on. People get 
this wrong all the time where they feel like they can only educate but never 
storytell. You can always do both at the same time.

Every single thing that we ever talk about always comes back to the main principle 
which is making your ideal reader the main character. Within the first one or two 
lines of a post, there always needs to be some financial incentive or some hint 
that this is going to make the user money. That's what we call unselfish content — 
how we make it all about them, even when you're talking about yourself.

**The 4-3-2-1 Framework**

4 posts a week. The split between the four:
- 1 post about education
- 1 post storytelling
- 1 post doing both (edu-telling using the SLAY framework)
- 1 post educating again

This immediately removes every single constraint of "what do I write about?" You 
go into a post knowing what type it is. You don't have to decide on the day.

The 3 is for three content themes or content pillars:
- Total Addressable Market content — something that goes viral because that's how 
  you increase followers. LinkedIn has 8 keywords it loves pushing: AI, 
  productivity, remote work, LinkedIn itself, and others. Use these as cheat codes.
- Growth content — stories linked to first principles. Quitting your job, building 
  a business, talking about money, these things.
- Sales content — how you actually monetize. Driving traffic to your main product 
  or offer.

The 2 is for two types of personas:
- ICP (Ideal Client Persona) — your actual buyer
- IFP (Ideal Follower Persona) — people who can't afford your product yet but will 
  push your content to your ICP. Don't ignore them. They're the ones that make you 
  look famous and in demand.

The 1 is one lead magnet every week. Lead magnets serve two purposes on LinkedIn:
1. You capture emails
2. They are some of the most viral formats on LinkedIn because people always want 
   free stuff. Consistently pushing lead magnets = people find you, follow you, 
   know you, trust you.

**On Authenticity with AI**

When we're using AI, when we're emulating other people's content, your content 
won't always sound like them as long as you prompt it correctly. Give context 
about you — your story, your beliefs, your social proof. That's what keeps it 
authentic rather than just generic AI fluff.

**On Images and Visual Content**

LinkedIn is a social media platform where you need to retain attention. A fast 
way to retain attention is by adding a graphic because when you put a graphic on 
your post, it takes over the entire feed. That gives you more time to win someone 
over and get them to actually read your post rather than just text-only content.

People need the extra validation that you're not just doing the thing, but you 
also ARE that thing.

**On Reverse Engineering Viral Content**

Find a viral post, even if it's not in your niche, and reverse engineer why it 
worked. Take the format, the hook style, the structure. Ask: how can I break down 
my topic using this same approach? Context is the winning format. Proof is your 
own story and experience. Then let AI do the final draft — it's 80% done. You 
go back and shift things to make it yours.

**Key Takeaways**
- Start with positioning: what do you want to be known for?
- Map your high value skills and monetizable expertise
- Use the 4-3-2-1 framework for consistent content output
- Always make the reader the main character
- Lead magnets every week — for emails AND virality
- Use AI for speed but ground everything in your own story
- Add images — they take over the feed and retain attention faster
- Reverse engineer viral formats from anyone, not just your niche
